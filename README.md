# Compliance Control Excel Optimization: Incident Dataset Aggregation & Audit

## 📌 Business Case & Overview
In retail banking, handling large volumes of regulatory incidents and internal disputes requires absolute precision. When processing raw transaction and dispute data across different clearings, compliance teams often face fragmented and unstructured datasets. Delays in cleaning and interpreting this data can lead to operational bottlenecks and missed deadlines under strict central bank mandates and payment infrastructure timelines.

This project showcases an **Advanced Excel Data Pipeline** developed to optimize the internal compliance control system. By aggregating, structured cleaning, and deep-diving into **incident datasets exceeding 100,000+ rows**, this framework eliminated processing overhead, streamlined operational audits, and ensured 100% data integrity for dynamic payment dispute (*Payment Disputes*) tracking and validation.

## 🎯 Key Objectives
* **Large-Scale Data Aggregation:** Consolidate disparate and unstructured compliance logs into a centralized analytical framework.
* **Data Cleansing & Normalization:** Design robust parsing logic to clean duplicates, handle missing values, and format messy data fields across 100k+ rows without losing critical audit trails.
* **Payment Disputes Audit:** Structure reporting attributes to align perfectly with international financial security standards and transaction dispute resolutions.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methods
* **Core Technology:** Microsoft Excel (Advanced Data Modeling, Pivot Tables, Complex Logic Formulas: `INDEX/MATCH`, `XLOOKUP`, nested logical operators, Array Formulas).
* **Data Processing Techniques:** Data imputation, deduplication, outlier detection, data normalization, time-series partitioning for incident tracking.
* **Domain Focus:** Banking Compliance Control, Risk Management, Payment Disputes, Risk Asset Protection.

## 📊 Operational & Analytical Logic
The optimization framework covers three essential phases of the compliance workflow:
1. **Raw Log Ingestion & Structural Clean:** Parsing messy multi-source incident tables, isolating true compliance bottlenecks, and establishing data consistency.
2. **Velocity & Metric Aggregation:** Utilizing optimized nested formulas to calculate end-to-end incident resolution times, financial loss exposures, and dispute categories.
3. **Audit Readiness:** Transforming raw rows into lean, auditable summaries that allow risk managers to immediately verify security perimeters and flag high-risk transactions. Detailed technical mechanics are documented in the `documentation/data_cleansing_methodology.md` folder.
